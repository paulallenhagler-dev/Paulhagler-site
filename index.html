What I actually do.

I’m not selling vague “AI transformation.” I look at the work people are already doing every day and remove the parts that waste time, get lost, or keep getting rebuilt by hand.

Fix messy workflows.

If customer information, orders, documents, or project details are scattered across email, spreadsheets, forms, and people’s heads, I help turn that mess into one clearer process.

Automate repetitive admin.

Follow-ups, data entry, reports, intake, status checks, research, handoffs, and recurring communication can often be automated without changing the entire business.

Build useful AI tools.

I build AI around real jobs: finding information, summarizing history, preparing work, checking for missing pieces, and helping people make decisions faster.
